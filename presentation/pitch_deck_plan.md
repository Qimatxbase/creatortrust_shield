## 3 Minute Speaker Script

Hi, we are pitching Creator Cyber Cover, a cyber insurance pilot for Australian content creators, streamers, esports players, influencers, and public figures.

Imagine a streamer has a sponsored tournament tomorrow. Overnight, their account is hacked, private details are leaked, and a scammer impersonates them to followers. They lose income, reputation, trust, and safety, and they do not know who to call. That is the customer problem we are solving.

For creators, a TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, or Instagram account is not just a profile. It is their storefront, workplace, payment channel, audience relationship, and reputation. When a cyber incident hits, it can cause income loss, sponsorship loss, privacy breach, reputation damage, family safety risk, and emotional stress.

This problem is backed by the broader cyber environment. The ASD ACSC Annual Cyber Threat Report 2024-25 reported more than 84,700 cybercrime reports, average reported individual losses of $33,000, average business losses of $80,850, and identity fraud as the top reported cybercrime.

We mapped the idea to IAG's customer journey. In Realise, customers say, "I think I need to protect the things I care about." For creators, that means realising their account, audience, and reputation are assets. In Explore, they ask, "What are my options?" They need simple choices and plain language. In Decide, they need confidence that the cover is right, the price feels fair, and they understand what is and is not covered.

Our solution is Creator Cyber Cover. It combines insurance with practical response support: account recovery, a cyber incident hotline, income loss cover, data breach support, doxxing response, scam and impersonation support, reputation support, and legal or takedown support.

Our Streamlit prototype shows the opportunity. The Market Opportunity tab shows creator and platform growth. The Cyber Risk tab shows national cyber risk and average losses. The Creator Impact tab shows how different cyber incidents affect income, sponsorships, reputation, privacy, safety, and emotional wellbeing.

We have used technology responsibly. The dashboard is decision-support, not a pricing engine. The dataset is synthetic for hackathon visualisation, and a real pilot would replace assumptions with verified market, claims, underwriting, and customer data.

The customer impact is clear. Before this product, creators may not know whether they are protected or who to call. After this product, they understand the risk, can choose clearer cover, and have support during an incident. That moves them from confused to confident across Realise, Explore, and Decide.

We also iterated. We narrowed the idea from broad cyber insurance to a creator-specific product, improved the source quality, simplified the dashboard, removed unnecessary sections, and focused the pitch on customer value and IAG's journey.

Our recommendation is to run a small pilot for Australian content creators and public figures. Test demand, quote completion, customer understanding, incident response usage, claims experience, and renewal intent. If the pilot works, IAG can scale a differentiated product for the next generation of digital income earners.

## 2 Minute Q&A Prep

### Why should IAG care about creators?
Creators are a growing digital-income segment with personal and small-business risk overlap. Their accounts, reputation, and sponsorship income function like business assets, but their insurance options may not be clear.

### How does this make the world safer?
It helps creators recover from cyber incidents, protects personal information and audience trust, and gives people practical support when online harm affects income and safety.

### Is the dashboard data official?
No. The CSV dataset is synthetic for hackathon visualisation. Public sources support the direction of the problem. A real pilot would use verified market, underwriting, claims, and customer data.

### Is this responsible technology?
Yes. We clearly disclose synthetic data, avoid pretending to price insurance, and use the dashboard as a decision-support prototype to test a product hypothesis.

### Why not just sell standard cyber insurance?
Creator risk is different. The key assets are account access, reputation, sponsorship income, audience trust, and personal safety. The product needs plain language, fast response, and tailored cover.

### What is the MVP?
A pilot product with account recovery, incident hotline, income interruption cover, data breach support, scam and impersonation support, and legal/takedown guidance.

### How would IAG measure success?
Quote completion, policy conversion, customer understanding, support usage, claims performance, customer satisfaction, and renewal intent.

### What did the team learn and improve?
We learned that the strongest idea is not generic cyber insurance. It is a focused product for creators, mapped to Realise, Explore, and Decide, supported by a simple dashboard and responsible data framing.

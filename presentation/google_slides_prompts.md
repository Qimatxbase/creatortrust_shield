# Google Slides Prompt Pack

Use these prompts to recreate the CreatorTrust Shield deck in Google Slides, Gemini, Canva, Gamma, or another slide-generation tool.

## Master Design Prompt

Create a premium hackathon pitch deck for **CreatorTrust Shield**, a cyber identity protection and insurance-support concept for Australian content creators.

Visual style:
- Senior designer quality, polished and presentation-ready.
- Dark cyber-insurance command-centre aesthetic.
- Background: near-black navy `#030812`.
- Panels/cards: dark navy `#0A1626` and `#0E2037`.
- Accent colours: cyan `#2CC9FF`, teal `#2DD4BF`, violet `#8B74F6`, rose `#FB6483`, amber `#F5A623`, green `#84CC16`.
- Typography: clean modern sans-serif, similar to Aptos, Inter, or Helvetica Neue.
- Use large bold headlines, short supporting text, and strong spacing.
- Avoid paragraphs on slides. Use visual hierarchy, cards, timelines, diagrams, and sparse labels.
- Add a thin vertical accent rail on the far left of each slide.
- Add small footer text: `CreatorTrust Shield` and slide number.
- Make every slide feel like a premium cyber product pitch, not a generic startup template.

Narrative:
The pitch should follow this sequence:
1. Hero
2. Customer problem
3. Why now
4. IAG journey fit
5. Solution model
6. Demo flow
7. Responsible technology
8. Pilot ask
9. Presenter notes

Important language:
- Use `CreatorTrust Shield`.
- Use IAG journey stages: `Realise`, `Explore`, `Decide`.
- Do not overclaim AI. Say simulated AI evidence / deterministic prototype analysis.
- Position the product as trusted creator registry + simulated monitoring + evidence-backed incident response.

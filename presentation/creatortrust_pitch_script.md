Product:
CreatorTrust Shield

One-line pitch:
CreatorTrust Shield protects content creators from deepfake, impersonation, and fake sponsorship attacks by combining a trusted creator registry, simulated monitoring, AI-assisted analysis, incident response, and future insurance assessment.

## Slide 4: Solution

Title:
CreatorTrust Shield

Speaker script:
CreatorTrust Shield is a cyber liability and digital identity protection solution for creators.

It starts with a trusted creator registry: official accounts, sponsors, original content, face and voice references, and approved campaigns. Then it monitors for suspicious public content, compares findings against the registry, runs simulated AI deepfake analysis, classifies risk, and generates evidence-backed response actions.

The key idea is important: we are not claiming to perfectly detect every deepfake. We are combining AI evidence with a trusted source of truth, which is more realistic and more useful for insurers.

## Q&A Prep

### Why is this a fit for IAG?
It sits at the intersection of cyber risk, identity protection, incident response, and future claims assessment. IAG can test a new digital-risk segment while helping customers feel safer online.

### Is the AI real?
No. The prototype uses simulated deterministic AI evidence. That is intentional and responsible for a hackathon. The point is to demonstrate the workflow, not to overclaim deepfake detection accuracy.

### What makes the solution different?
It does not rely only on generic AI detection. It uses a trusted creator registry as the source of truth, then compares suspicious content against registered accounts, sponsors, original content, and identity references.

### What is the MVP?
A creator registry, metadata content registration, simulated monitoring, registry comparison, simulated AI evidence, risk classification, incident report, and response actions.

### How would success be measured?
Creator registration rate, scan-to-incident conversion, incident response completion, creator trust score, sponsor confidence, support usage, and future insurance claim assessment quality.

### What did the team improve?
We moved from a broad cyber insurance idea to a more concrete creator identity-protection workflow. We clarified the journey, reduced overclaims, made the demo deterministic, and aligned the product to IAG's Realise, Explore, and Decide stages.
